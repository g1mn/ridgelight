Lunar Window

Lunar South Pole Landing Site Navigator

Pick a lunar south pole site and see when the Sun and Earth rise and set there, with the terrain checked against NASA data.

Data sources: NASA JPL Horizons ephemerides, LOLA illumination products, LROC imagery.
QuickMap and Moon Trek, external, open in a new tab and not called by this page: LROC QuickMap, NASA Moon Trek.
